About PSUT.

Founded in 1991 in Amman, Princess Sumaya University for Technology is the academic arm of the Royal Scientific Society, bringing education, research and technology together.

A foundation in technology.

PSUT began with a single bachelor’s degree in Computer Science. It has since grown to offer undergraduate and postgraduate study across computing, engineering and business.

Learning that looks ahead.

From software engineering and communications to data science, artificial intelligence and cyber security, the university’s development reflects the changing world of technology.

Knowledge with purpose.

As part of the Royal Scientific Society’s academic mission, PSUT connects scientific knowledge with technical skills, research and innovation, preparing graduates to contribute in Jordan and beyond.

A university in the heart of Jordan.
A community looking ahead.
